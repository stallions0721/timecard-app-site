Ver. 3.6.0 November 2026

Register several breaks as time ranges

  • Add multiple start–end ranges. Time between 10 PM and 5 AM is counted as a late-night break.
  • Breaks that cross midnight use Next day on the time picker.
  • Entering a duration only, as before, still works.

Insert notes with one tap

  • Add shortcuts such as half day or remote, then tap to insert them.
  • Paid leave stays fixed on the left. Other shortcuts can be added, renamed, deleted, and reordered.
  • Added shortcuts are notes only. Pay and paid-leave balance do not change.

Six-week months stay readable on short screens

The monthly calendar tightens spacing when a month needs six weeks. Narrow cells hide extra hours and keep the clock-in/out range. Crashes when picking a time, checking backup, or showing ads are also fixed.

See Daily in the user guide for the steps.

Ver. 3.5.2 November 2026

Widget date change and clock-in edits

  • After clock-out, you can clock in from the widget the next day without opening the app.
  • Editing clock-in time before clock-out no longer clears it.

See See punches and shifts from a Home Screen widget for widget setup.

Ver. 3.5.1 November 2026

Shift calendar layout matches the app

  • Memos support line breaks (up to 3 lines with one shift, 1 line with two or more).
  • App time display (range / hours) is reflected on the widget.
  • In Japanese, holiday names stay visible even when the widget is smaller.

Android widgets are easier to use

  • Shift calendar defaults to about 4×4 with larger type.
  • Set background opacity in Home & widget settings.
  • 1×1 and 2×1 punch widgets are less likely to clip times.
Ver. 3.5.0 November 2026

Add a shift calendar to the Home Screen

  • On iPhone, medium shows this week and large shows this month.
  • Tap a day to open it.
  • Month and Year can be grouped by workplace or by cutoff day.
  • Estimated pay on the widget can be shown or hidden.

See See punches and shifts from a Home Screen widget and Group monthly totals by cutoff day or workplace.

Ver. 3.4.0 September 2026

Calendar time ranges and planned shifts

  • The monthly calendar can show each day's start-to-end time range.
  • Switch between time range and hours worked from the Month menu.
  • Turn on planned shifts to save future days as plans. Off keeps the previous behavior.
  • Planned days use a dashed chip on the calendar and a P badge in the list.
  • You can replace planned-only days with actual times from the workplace schedule, after you confirm.
  • Japanese public holiday names are hidden when the app language is English.
Ver. 3.3.0 September 2026

Date format and calendar stability

  • You can choose how dates appear on screen.
  • After updating, your current date order stays the same until you change it in settings.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur around certain dates in the monthly calendar.
  • Clearer one-time tips for the calendar, the ad-free period, and the date format.
Ver. 3.2.0 July 2026

Monthly calendar and ad-free time

  • Added a calendar view on the monthly screen, with switching to the list.
  • Watch a short rewarded video to hide in-app ads for a limited time.
  • Holiday names and bulk workplace registration work in calendar view.
Ver. 3.1.3 June 2026

Ad placement

  • Adjusted ad placement on the home, monthly, and yearly screens.
  • Improved where monthly list ads appear, including near month-end.
  • Improved when ads load, so they show more reliably.
Ver. 3.1.2 June 2026

Clock-out after overnight shifts

  • Fixed an issue where clock-out could fail after overnight shifts that cross midnight.
  • Adjusted when ads appear after clock-out to reduce accidental taps.
  • Improved ad loading on the monthly list.
Ver. 3.1.1 June 2026

Launch, daily entry, and the widget

  • Fixed an issue where the app could stay on the splash screen at launch.
  • Home widget punches on Android can vibrate and show a success message. Vibration can be turned off in settings.
  • Fixed monthly bulk actions that were hard to use on small screens.
  • Choose whether to stay on the daily screen or return to the monthly list after save. The default is to stay.
  • The weekday is shown in the daily screen title.
  • Clearer labels and layout for yearly and monthly figures.
  • The iPhone version is on the App Store and can take over data from Android.
Ver. 3.1.0 May 2026

Yearly view, estimated net pay, and paid leave

  • Yearly view switches between hours, income, and summary, and adds a monthly table. Tap a month to open it.
  • Estimated net pay for the year and the current month. Tax and social-insurance settings are available in the Japanese UI.
  • Paid leave: set granted days on a workplace, record a day, and see the remaining balance on the yearly summary.
  • The daily screen shows hours and estimated pay more clearly, including a next-day end time.
  • Leave types, such as paid leave, can be chosen on the daily screen.
Ver. 3.0.11 May 2026

Pay display and moving between days

  • Monthly and daily pay display follows the previous app, and the gap between the breakdown and the total is smaller.
  • Swipe, or use the arrows, on the daily screen to move to the previous or next day.
  • Home shows progress toward the monthly goal, and explains how holidays are counted.
  • The daily screen layout is tidier, including notes, a single-line memo, and help tips.
Ver. 3.0.10 May 2026

Share the Otsukare card

  • You can share the Otsukare card as an image. The caption and hashtag can be changed in Settings.
  • Otsukare card options are under Settings: on or off, theme, and whether the monthly goal appears on the card.
  • The monthly list is easier to scan, and the pay-calculation note moved to the monthly totals screen.
Ver. 3.0.0–3.0.9 April–May 2026

Early updates

Main changes from April through May 2026.

  • A holiday-work flag can be set on the daily screen.
  • Workplaces you no longer use can be hidden from the list.
  • Month and Year can use a range that follows the cutoff day.
  • On Android, you can choose Classic or Modern appearance.
  • Notes and the pay breakdown on the monthly list are easier to read.
  • Monthly export supports PDF as well as CSV.
  • Work patterns can be turned off, and the monthly list layout was tidied.
