User guide

Screens and basic operations

This guide explains how to use Time Card, a time-tracking app for part-time workers, freelancers, and others paid by the hour. Menu names below match the English app UI (labels differ in Japanese).

Getting started

Time Card records when you work on your device and shows estimated pay based on hourly settings. It is meant for personal use, not as a replacement for a company-wide attendance system.

  1. Install the app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the Settings tab → Workplaces and register a workplace name, hourly wage, and other options (a setup dialog may appear on first launch).
  3. On the Home tab, tap Clock in / Clock out to record your work.
  4. On the Monthly tab, review hours and estimated pay for the month.

Data is stored on your device by default. Before changing phones, use Backup & restore (described below).

Home — punch in/out

Clock in and clock out

  • Tap the large center button to clock in or clock out (the label changes with your current status).
  • Use the Workplace dropdown to choose which workplace to record under when you have several registered.
  • You can enter a Notes memo. It is saved when you leave the field (sometimes available before clock-in).

Other actions

  • Correct clock-in time — Adjust the most recent punch time.
  • Edit today — Open the daily detail screen to edit start/end times, breaks, and more.
  • ? at the top — Explains how weekend/holiday punches affect totals (when using Japanese UI for holidays; see in-app help).
  • This month’s goal — If a goal amount is set for the workplace, a progress card appears. Tap to expand details.

Monthly — review a month

The Monthly tab shows a list and summary for the selected month.

  • Change year/month and summary scope (e.g. all workplaces, closing day) at the top, depending on workplace settings.
  • The summary shows days, hours worked, overtime, late night, total pay (estimate), and more.
  • Tap a date to open daily detail for that day.
  • Tap Details for a breakdown (totals and per-workplace).
  • You may move to the previous or next month with arrows or swipe.
  • Use + to add a record manually.

Yearly — annual trends

The Yearly tab shows estimated income and goal progress for the year.

  • Yearly summary — Year-to-date total, annual goal, and progress.
  • Monthly income chart — Estimated income per month and cumulative trend.
  • Goal simulation — Year-end projection at your current pace and pace needed to reach your goal.

Daily — edit one day

Fix punches or enter a full day manually. Open from a date on the monthly list or from Edit today on Home.

  • Select Workplace and Work pattern.
  • Tap Start / End times to change them. Use the Next day toggle for shifts that end after midnight.
  • Set Regular break and Late-night break.
  • Leave flag — Tap ? next to the label for an explanation.
  • Notes (single line) and tags such as early leave may be available.
  • Tap Save at the top to apply. If you change the date with unsaved edits, you will be asked to confirm.
  • Swipe left/right or use side arrows to move to the previous or next day.

Workplaces

Go to Settings → Workplaces to list, add, and edit workplaces.

  • Tap + to add a workplace.
  • For each workplace, set hourly wage, rounding (e.g. per minute), closing day, goal amount, default start/end times, break times, and more.
  • Register separate workplaces if you work multiple jobs or sites.

Work patterns

Under Settings → Work patterns, register rules for shifts or pay rates and select them on the daily screen. Details depend on how you configure each pattern and workplace.

Home screen widget

On iPhone and Android you can add a home screen widget to punch in/out without opening the full app (steps and appearance vary by OS and device).

  1. Add the Time Card widget from your device’s home screen settings.
  2. In the app: Settings → Home screen widget — choose automatic or fixed workplace for punches, and whether to show estimated monthly pay on the widget.
  3. Use the widget buttons to clock in or out.

Widget layout and size follow your OS. If the widget does not work as expected, use the Home tab inside the app.

Backup & restore

Settings → Backup & restore lets you back up data when you choose. Nothing is sent to the cloud automatically.

  • Android — Back up and restore via Google Drive (Google sign-in may be required).
  • iPhone — Back up and restore via iCloud (iPhone to iPhone). To move from Android to iPhone, check the in-app instructions for Google Drive.
  • We recommend backing up before changing devices or reinstalling the app.

Export & share (CSV)

From Settings → Mail delivery settings (and related screens), you can export monthly data and share it via Mail, Files, or other apps. Follow the on-screen instructions for format and destination.

Sharing uses your device’s apps only. The developer does not receive your exported files or email content.

FAQ

Is the pay shown my final salary?
No. It is an estimate from your hourly settings. Actual pay and invoicing follow your employer or contract.
I punched the wrong time
Use Correct clock-in time on Home, or open Daily detail for that day, fix start/end times, and tap Save.
What happens if I delete the app?
Data on the device may be lost. Back up first using Backup & restore.
How do I switch to Japanese?
Open Settings → App settings and change the app or device language if available.
