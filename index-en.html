FAQ

Common questions about this free time-tracking app. For step-by-step help, see the user guide.

Is Time Card free?

Yes. Download it for free from the App Store and Google Play. The app shows ads. Watch a short rewarded video to hide in-app ads for 48 hours.

Can I use it for part-time or freelance work?

Yes. It is not an enterprise attendance system. It helps individuals record when they worked and see monthly hours and pay estimates. Built for part-time, freelance, and hourly jobs.

Can I register multiple workplaces?

Yes. Set hourly rate, payday, rounding, and goals per workplace. Switch the workplace on the Home screen before punching.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Available on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). The Android version has 500k+ installs.

Where is punch data stored?

Time records stay on your device. There is no developer server that stores attendance data. Google Drive or iCloud backup is optional.

Does it calculate pay?

It shows pay estimates from your hourly settings. Final pay and invoices follow your employer or contract.

Can I see the month as a calendar?

Yes. The Month tab switches between a list and a calendar. The calendar shows daily hours, holiday names, and total pay.

See how it works

From one-tap punches to the monthly list and calendar, yearly income chart, workplaces, and widget. The UI may change with updates.

01 · Home

One-tap clock-in and clock-out

Use the large button on Home. You can see today’s status and start/end times at a glance.

  • Punch in one tap. Add breaks or notes only when you need them
  • Switch workplaces before you punch
  • Open Edit today to fix times later

02 · Monthly list

Hours and estimated pay for the month

Days, hours, overtime, late night, and total pay sit at the top. Tap a date to edit that day.

  • IN, OUT, break, and total for each day
  • Overtime, late night, and notes on the same row
  • Open Details for a per-workplace breakdown

03 · Calendar NEW

See the whole month at a glance

The same totals are available in calendar view. Switch from the icons on the Month tab or in Settings.

  • Daily hours and workplace icons on each date
  • Holiday names when the app language is Japanese
  • The same pay and overtime summary as the list

04 · Daily

Fix times, breaks, and notes

Correct a punch or enter a day by hand. Total hours and estimated pay update immediately.

  • Start, end, regular break, and late-night break
  • Work patterns, leave flag, and quick tags
  • Shifts that end the next day

05 · Year

Income chart and yearly goals

Compare monthly income with the year-to-date total, and see how you track against a monthly goal pace.

  • Switch Hours, Income, and Summary
  • Monthly bars plus a cumulative line
  • Dashed line for monthly goal pace

06 · Workplaces

Separate rates for each job

Cafe, night shift, agency work — set hourly rate and rounding per workplace.

  • Hourly rate, payday, rounding, and goals per site
  • Choose the workplace on Home before you punch
  • See a per-workplace breakdown in Month details

07 · Widget

Punch from the home screen. Hide pay if you want

Choose automatic or a fixed workplace for widget punches. Monthly pay can stay masked with ***.

  • Automatic uses the top workplace; or pick one
  • Hide monthly pay on the widget for privacy
  • Layout and steps vary by device and OS
