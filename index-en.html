For part-time, freelance & hourly work

Track when you worked — easily

Log clock-in and clock-out every day. Supports multiple workplaces, breaks, overnight shifts, and crossing midnight. View monthly and yearly summaries and estimated pay based on your hourly rate — all on your device.

Features

Built for individuals who want to see “today” and “this month” — not enterprise workforce management.

Clock in/out, breaks & notes

Punch from the home screen. Supports breaks, memos, and shifts that span dates or midnight.

Multiple workplaces

Set hourly rate, pay period, rounding, and target amounts per workplace.

Monthly & yearly views

Monthly hours, overtime, estimated pay, breakdown by workplace, yearly charts, and goal simulation.

Home screen widgets

Punch from your device home screen (availability varies by device and OS).

Optional backup

Backup and restore via Google Drive or iCloud; share CSV through your device’s share sheet (e.g. email).

Data stays on your device

Time records are not stored on the developer’s servers. Cloud backup only when you choose it.

Screenshots

From punching in to monthly and yearly summaries, daily edits, and workplace settings. UI may change with updates.

Data & ads

  • Most time-tracking data is stored on your device.
  • Release builds show ads (Google AdMob). Firebase Crashlytics is used to improve stability.
